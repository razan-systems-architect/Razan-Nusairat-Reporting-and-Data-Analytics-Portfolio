##National Accreditation Standards for the Inclusion of Children with ASD within the Mainstream Education System of Jordan
Executive Summary

Organization: Health Care Accreditation Council (HCAC)
Funder: European Union (EU)
Role: Guideline Development Officer
Duration: 4–5 months, 2021
Deliverable: 97-page national accreditation standards framework

Developed a national, evidence-based accreditation framework to support the inclusion of children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) within Jordan's mainstream education system.

The framework translated Jordanian legislation, national inclusive-education policy, and international evidence and standards into operational accreditation criteria, measurable indicators, quality requirements, and information-management specifications for schools and education stakeholders.

The work required synthesizing legal, clinical, educational, and operational evidence into a structured framework that could support school accreditation, monitoring, quality improvement, and future data-driven reporting.

1. Context & Operational Challenge

Jordan had established legislative commitments to inclusive education, but schools lacked standardized operational criteria for implementing and monitoring the inclusion of children with ASD.

The project addressed this gap by defining what inclusive practice should look like, how it should be assessed, and what information should be captured to monitor implementation and quality.

2. Framework Architecture

I developed a 97-page accreditation framework organized across eight domains, translating policy and evidence into operational standards and measurable criteria.

Domain	Focus
1. Leadership & Governance	Institutional accountability, strategic planning, and compliance
2. Rights & Legislation	Non-discrimination, safeguarding, consent, and student rights
3. Teachers & Staff	Competency frameworks, training, and professional standards
4. Inclusive Educational Services	IEP processes, multidisciplinary support, and curriculum adaptation
5. Inclusive Environment	Accessibility, sensory considerations, assistive resources, and safety
6. Community & Family Engagement	Awareness, parental participation, and stigma reduction
7. Quality Improvement	Performance monitoring, satisfaction, evaluation, and grievance mechanisms
8. Information Management	Data requirements, documentation, privacy, and information-system considerations

The framework incorporated 50+ operational criteria spanning governance, educational delivery, accessibility, quality improvement, safeguarding, and information management.

3. Data & Information Architecture

A key component of the framework was defining the information requirements needed to monitor implementation and quality over time.

This included specifications for:

Student enrollment, attendance, transfers, and dropout monitoring
Disability-related disaggregation and demographic indicators
Assessment and student-support documentation
Individualized Education Program (IEP) monitoring
Assistive-resource and accessibility inventories
Staff competency and training records
Parent/student satisfaction and feedback
Complaints, grievances, and resolution tracking
Quality-improvement indicators

The framework was designed with future integration with Jordan's national Education Management Information System (EMIS) in mind, in coordination with relevant education-sector stakeholders. It defined information requirements and monitoring structures rather than developing the EMIS itself.

4. Methodology

The framework was developed through synthesis of:

Jordanian legislation and national education policy
UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD)
UNESCO and UNICEF guidance and evidence
Autism and inclusive-education research
Accreditation and quality-assurance principles
Operational requirements of schools and education stakeholders

The resulting framework converted high-level policy commitments into operational standards and measurable requirements that could be applied within educational institutions.

5. Impact

The project established a unified operational framework for ASD-inclusive education accreditation and quality monitoring in Jordan, providing a structured basis for:

School accreditation and assessment
Ministry-level monitoring and inspection
Quality-improvement planning
Standardized documentation and information collection
Stakeholder accountability
Future data-driven monitoring and reporting
EU-funded program reporting
Portfolio Evidence

This repository contains a sanitized architectural summary and presentation of the framework.
