# National Accreditation Standards for the Inclusion of Children with ASD within the Mainstream Education System of Jordan

**Organization:** Health Care Accreditation Council (HCAC)  
**Funder:** European Union (EU)  
**Role:** Guideline Development Officer  
**Duration:** 4–5 months | **Deliverable:** 97-page national framework

## Problem

Jordan had legislative commitments to inclusive education, but schools lacked standardized operational standards and measurable criteria for implementing and monitoring the inclusion of children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) in mainstream education.

## Solution

Developed a **97-page national accreditation framework** structured across 8 domains and 50+ measurable criteria:

- Leadership & Governance
- Rights & Legislation
- Teachers & Staff
- Inclusive Educational Services
- Inclusive Environment
- Community & Family Engagement
- Quality Improvement
- Information Management

The framework translated national legislation, inclusive-education policy, and international evidence into practical accreditation and quality requirements for schools.

## Method

Synthesized **Jordanian legislation, national education policy, UN CRPD, UNESCO and UNICEF guidance, and ASD/inclusive-education research** into an operational standards framework.

The information-management component defined requirements for student tracking, assessment, IEP monitoring, staff training, resources, satisfaction, grievances, and quality indicators, with consideration for **future integration with Jordan's national Education Management Information System (EMIS)**.

## Impact

Established a unified operational framework to support **school accreditation, Ministry-level monitoring, quality improvement, stakeholder accountability, and future data-driven reporting** for ASD-inclusive education in Jordan.

The original 97-page framework is not publicly reproduced due to institutional confidentiality. This repository provides a sanitized presentation and architectural summary of the work.
